# BarkLog standing design rules

1. The owner repeatedly requested more colour, character and interactive motion. Every feature
   needs a considered composition and a purposeful interaction; neutral form wireframes alone
   are insufficient. Use the same BarkLog kit, not unrelated feature brands.
2. Mobbin informs structure and mechanics. Keep evidence in private local Research cards with
   canonical links. Product artwork, palette and compositions remain BarkLog's own.
3. A care note is first-class content: writing room, optional context, a recoverable draft,
   a visible save action, and saved notes readable in detail and Timeline.
4. The owner selected the current web board exactly on 2026-10-16: Bricolage Grotesque
   700/800 for display/numbers, Inter 400/500 for reading. This supersedes Inter-only.
   Match computed hierarchy, scale, line length and spacing in native; do not simplify.
   Firm controls stay pinned; only a touched or earned object may spring. Readable content uses curves.
5. No decorative gradients or card shadows. Colour includes labels, symbols or exact values.
6. Each proposal needs light/dark, larger-text, reduced-motion and real screenshot review.
   The approved native export and implementation claims change only through a later approved slice.
7. Detailed onboarding is optional depth, not a longer gate. Keep name-only fast start; reuse
   every optional answer, keep practice outside the journal, and request permissions only in context.
8. Every screen should have a small, readable text entrance. A touch should answer visibly:
   buttons give and show one soft bubble; sliders track the finger and settle once. Keep the
   effect short, avoid repeated loops, and land immediately when reduced motion is on.
9. Home's illustration is one dog on a transparent ground. Give it a separate space beside
   the count; status and the day line sit below. No scenery may sit behind care facts.
10. The first-run dog card uses the same vector character as Home. Keep one dog, one name,
    a plain ground and separate moving parts. Do not leave the older park picture in previews.
11. Each saved care log earns a short dog reaction on Home. Quick logs react on landing;
    detailed logs react once when Home returns. Keep it scoped to the selected dog, skip
    failed/cancelled saves and early Undo, and retain written feedback with reduced motion.
12. A working feature is not a matching screen. Keep the current board as reference;
    task022 tracks every product screen/state individually. Require same-fixture
    board/native comparison, intermediate motion frames and individual owner review
    before calling it matched. Do not alter the board to hide a native mismatch.
13. Later 2026-10-16 the owner requested a separate account/Premium-AI board chapter.
    Required login follows demonstrated value; the journal remains free after sign-in.
    Prices stay undecided. This proposal is in `premium/`; it does not modify the
    earlier reference or authorize native integrations. Keep access optional, deny
    paths usable, AI context explicit and the free choice visible.

14. The new proposal defaults to Light. Useful dog details are optional and editable;
    use multi-select where answers can coexist, and always allow custom/mixed breed
    entry. Notification switches reflect returned permission and reminder state.
    AI receives profile/care notes only after a separate explicit selection, retaining
    the source snapshot. Motion must retain immediate reduced-motion states.

15. In the new Home proposal, the mascot, care count and named/time care strip
    belong in one card. The dog needs its own space, with no overlap on care facts.
    Varied mascot idle actions settle with quiet gaps; saved-log feedback takes
    priority. Stop offscreen/disposed motion and honour reduced motion immediately.

16. The dog stays on transparent ground during taps/hover/focus too. No filled
    rectangular interaction tint behind its artwork (owner feedback, 2026-10-17).
17. The mascot authoring/playback workflow must use free/open-source tools and
    original artwork. Do not introduce paid export requirements, subscriptions
    or mandatory third-party branding without the owner's request (2026-10-17).
18. The owner subsequently selected Rive and explicitly allowed a new dog for
    task031 (2026-10-17). Original Rive authoring is now authorized for this study;
    retain its actual workspace/export restrictions. The new character still
    needs review before native adoption. Keep the dog transparent and preserve
    the existing Home composition and separate care facts.

19. On 2026-10-17 the owner activated Draco Arts Studios Cadet and authorized the
    approved Rive spaniel in the native application (task032). Use the clean
    official export; retain the editable source. Preserve transparent presses,
    reduced motion, quiet varied idles and post-commit save reactions.

20. The owner changed the output boundary on 2026-10-17: mobile reports and raw
    CSV remain free; report/Memory Book PDF, printing and PDF sharing are Premium.
    Restore the personalized Home Ask entry. Offer upgrades at requested outputs,
    keep an obvious return to the preview, and do not interrupt care logging.
    Photo-film export is a proposed paid feature; its new editor requires review
    before native integration. Prices and real billing remain deferred.

21. On 19 October the owner liked the existing chat layout and text style but
    requested structured answer cards with question-relevant saved logs. Keep
    the compact shell whole. Separate saved facts and general guidance; never
    turn counts or missing records into a health/wellbeing assessment. Reserve
    context-relevant dog-product placement for a later phase, absent by default.
22. The same owner's later clarification changes the new AI context direction:
    after explicit consent to this scope, include the current dog's name/profile
    and the past seven days automatically. Users review context rather than
    selecting individual logs. This supersedes rule 14's manual-selection design
    for task041; existing native consent must not silently expand. All app-facing
    names say BarkLog AI. Atlas remains an internal service identifier only.
