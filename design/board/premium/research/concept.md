# BarkLog · care, understood

Owner-requested board direction, 16 October 2026. Design proposal only. Prices stay
undecided, per the owner’s reply. Shared Compose and the installed app are unchanged.

1. **The angle.** Keep the small facts of your dog’s day, then ask AI to help put
   the records you choose into plain words.
2. **Who it is for.** A caregiver who logs a meal or walk while doing it and later
   wants to remember what happened, prepare a question or keep a memory.
3. **The promise.** After a week of logging, they can find their actual observations
   by date; Premium can summarize that selected context and link back to it. No
   promise of better health, fewer appointments or a complete picture of the dog.
4. **The hero object.** The same BarkLog dog and a dated care record: opening →
   identity → practice → account → plan → Home → source-backed conversation.
5. **The feature map.** The three promise carriers are **one-tap local logging**,
   **findable dated records**, and **Premium AI conversation with explicit context**.
   Free tools also include notes, factual charts, reminders, health record organization,
   training, photos, expenses, Memory Book and CSV/PDF exports. Proposed paid AI
   additions are generated recaps, appointment-question drafts and memory captions.
6. **What it is not.** Not a veterinarian or diagnostic tool; AI must not prescribe.
   Not a cloud journal backup or household-sharing service. Login identifies access
   and future entitlements; it does not imply care records are backed up.
7. **Proof the numbers are honest.** The example day contains seven records from
   `B.example`, including meals at 08:15/17:45 and a walk at 18:20. The assistant
   computes its count and source list from the selected in-memory records. Practice
   stays separate. The 16–23 October trial is a labeled seven-day design scenario;
   no trial can be offered before the actual price, eligibility and store product
   are set. No fabricated reviews, savings, medical results or conversion forecasts.
