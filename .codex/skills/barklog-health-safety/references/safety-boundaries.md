# BarkLog Safety Boundaries

## Risk Tiers

### Low

- event logging
- timeline summaries
- neutral history views

### Medium

- trend summaries
- symptom clustering
- wellness narratives

### High

- triage questionnaires
- toxicity lookup or camera identification
- emergency SOS guidance
- first-aid instructions

## Allowed Behavior

- summarize observed facts
- call out uncertainty
- recommend escalation pathways
- emphasize emergency contacts and veterinary support

## Avoid

- claiming diagnosis
- implying treatment certainty
- hiding urgent steps behind long flows
- using playful or decorative tone in emergency contexts
- paywalling urgent first guidance

## Copy Direction

- factual and calm
- explicit about uncertainty
- action-oriented when urgency is possible
- clear about when to seek immediate professional help
